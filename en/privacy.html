Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the Qaat (قاعات) app and website collect, why, how we protect it, and your rights.

What we collectHow we use itWho we share it with SecurityRetentionYour rightsContact
In short: we collect only what is needed for browsing, booking and notifications. We do not sell your data, we do not collect your location or contacts, and we never receive payments or card details — deposits are paid directly to the venue owner.

Who we are

Qaat is a platform connecting people looking for wedding halls, condolence halls, villas, chalets and event services with their owners. We are responsible for the data collected by the app. See Contact to reach us.

What we don't collect: your location, contacts, messages, camera or microphone, or bank card details. The app asks only for notification permission; owners grant photo-library access when choosing photos of their venue.

How we use data

  • To show venues available in your area and date, and keep your favorites.
  • To deliver your booking request to the owner, track its stages, and send related notifications and reminders.
  • To verify your phone number by sending a code, or by receiving a WhatsApp message from you.
  • To greet you by name and pre-fill the booking form.
  • To show ads based on area and section only (we don't build an advertising profile of you and use no third-party ad networks).
  • For aggregate statistics to improve the app and to show owners and advertisers view and click counts (totals, without identities).
  • To understand which devices and app versions are in use, so we can plan updates.
  • To prevent abuse (such as a daily limit on booking requests) and protect accounts.

Who we share data with

  • The owner of the venue you book: sees only your name, contact phone and request details, to contact you about the booking.
  • Service providers that run the app for us:
    • Supabase — database and photo storage, hosted in Ireland (European Union).
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivering notifications to your device.
    • Meta (WhatsApp Business) — verification-code and alert messages.
  • Where required by law, or to protect users from fraud or harm.

We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

How we protect your data

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Access rules are enforced inside the database itself: each user sees only their own data, and owners see only requests for their own venue.
  • Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on your phone and excluded from backups.
  • Every administrative action is recorded in an audit log.

Retention

  • Account and booking data: as long as your account exists; deleted when you delete your account.
  • Detailed usage logs: about 400 days, after which only daily aggregate totals remain (no identifiers).
  • Owners' subscription and payment records: as long as needed for accounting purposes.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account and data. See Delete your account. You can turn off notifications at any time in your phone settings.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) because it involves bookings and financial commitments. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The new version is published here with its date, and we'll notify you in the app if a change is significant.
