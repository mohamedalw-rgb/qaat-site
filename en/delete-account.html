Delete your account and data

The احجزلي (by Qaat) app — you can request deletion of your account and all your personal data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a WhatsApp message from the phone number registered to your account to +972 52-488-8190 saying: “Please delete my احجزلي account”.
  2. Or email qaat26.app@gmail.com with your name and registered phone number; we'll verify you own the number before deleting.
  3. We complete the request within 30 days at most and confirm when it's done.
If you have an active booking (pending or confirmed), we delete the account once it is cancelled or its date has passed, so the other party isn't affected.

What is deleted

  • Your name, phone numbers, town and device information.
  • Favorites, notifications and your notification token.
  • Booking requests and their details linked to your account.
  • For owners: listings and their photos, after active bookings are settled.

What may be retained

  • Aggregate statistics (such as a hall's daily view count) that carry no identifier of you.
  • Owners' subscription payment records, for as long as needed for accounting or legal obligations.

Deleting only some data

You can also ask us to delete specific data (such as device information or a note on a past booking) without deleting the whole account, using the same method above.
