How booking works

Simple and transparent, from first request to confirmation.

Choose area and date

Only venues free on that day are shown, so you don't waste time calling around.

Send a request

Your name, phone and guest count. The owner is notified instantly and reminded until they reply.

Confirm with a deposit

The owner accepts, you pay the deposit directly to them, and you follow every step under “My bookings”.

For hall, villa and service owners

Put your venue in front of customers searching in your area, and manage booking requests from your phone.

  • Your own in-app dashboard: requests, photos, closed days and listing details.
